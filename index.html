about me

Hi again!

I'm Michelle, a Multimedia Production student based in Germany, with a particular interest in storytelling and creative experiments.

I've always been curious about how things come together. One idea might turn into a publication, another into a short film, or even something edible. I enjoy exploring different mediums, experimenting with possibilities, and figuring things out along the way.

DSCVRMI is my little archive of those explorations. A collection of things I've made, ideas I've pursued, and the occasional rabbit hole I've happily fallen into.
