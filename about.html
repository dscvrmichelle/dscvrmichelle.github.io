little about me

I'm Michelle, a multimedia production student based in Germany with an interest in visual storytelling, editorial design, and concept development.

My work moves between different formats, from zines and websites to film and physical objects. I enjoy exploring different mediums and figuring out which one works best for the idea I'm trying to communicate.

how i approach things

Most of my ideas begin with curiosity. Sometimes it's a question, sometimes a random observation, and sometimes it's simply something I can't stop thinking about.

I like collecting references, exploring connections, and developing concepts before deciding what the final outcome should look like.
