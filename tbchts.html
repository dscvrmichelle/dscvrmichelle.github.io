The Body Cannot Hold The Sky

An exploration of someone's presence through poetry, small details, and the universe.

01 / THE STORY

about the project

I made this zine about someone I barely knew at the time.

It started with a poem I had written for my blog. I wasn't entirely sure what I was feeling when I wrote it, but I knew I wanted to do something more with it. The poem mentioned the universe, and coincidentally, one of the few things I knew about this person was their birthday. That got me thinking: how much could I actually tell about someone's existence with so little information?

I started digging around and found NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day from the date they were born, along with a map of the night sky from that same day. I also collected illustrations of little things that reminded me of our interactions, most of which probably wouldn't mean much to anyone else.

Somewhere along the way, I began writing more poems to accompany the visuals. What was initially supposed to be a way of giving an old poem a new form slowly turned into a whole new piece of its own.

Looking back, I find it quite funny that I managed to make something so personal about someone I barely knew. But I think that's also what I like about this project. I didn't have much to work with, and somehow, that was enough.
