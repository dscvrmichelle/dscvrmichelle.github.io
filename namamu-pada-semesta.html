Namamu Pada Semesta

publication / zine

about the project

Namamu Pada Semesta began with a poem. At the time, I knew very little about the person who inspired it, but somehow that limited information became the starting point for something much more elaborate.

I began exploring how small details, fragments of our interactions, and even something as simple as a birthday could be connected to the idea of the universe. The concept gradually expanded from the poem into a visual narrative, bringing together poetry, symbolism, and personal observations.
